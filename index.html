The Book

David and the Really Big Problem

📖 Faith-Based 🌊 Courage 👨‍👩‍👦 Ages 5 to 9 📜 1 Samuel 17 🏫 Anti-Bullying

Micah loves school: spelling games, drawing superheroes, and being with his friends. But lately, one part of school makes his stomach feel twisty. A boy named Jayden has been bothering him, and no matter how hard Micah tries to ignore it, the problem keeps growing.

That evening, Mom opens the Bible and shows Micah the story of David, a boy who also faced something much bigger than himself. David was younger and smaller than Goliath. But he didn't give up because he trusted God.

The next morning, Micah goes back to school with something new: the courage to do what is right, even when he is afraid.

"The battle is the LORD's…"
1 Samuel 17:47, KJV

Why This Book

Written for the Real Giants Kids Face Today

Every element of this book is designed to help children understand that courage is not the absence of fear. It is trusting God and choosing what is right anyway.

📖

Rooted in Scripture

Directly inspired by 1 Samuel 17. David and Goliath retold through the lens of a modern child's daily life.

🌊

Tackles Real Issues

Bullying, fear, and feeling small are real. This book names them honestly and points children to a real God who sees them.

👨‍👩‍👦

Diverse & Relatable

Characters and families that reflect the real world children live in, so every child sees themselves in the story.

💬

Starts Conversations

Designed to open doors between parents, teachers, pastors, and children about the hard stuff, with Scripture as the foundation.

🙏🏾

Faith That Works

Shows children that prayer is real, that God hears them, and that courage is something God gives, not something you have to find alone.

✍🏾

Crafted With Care

Every word chosen to be accessible to ages 5 to 9 while meaningful enough for parents and teachers to read aloud with depth.

About the Author

A. S. Gates

A. S. Gates is a faith-based children's book author on a mission to help young readers see that they are never alone, no matter how big their problem feels.

Through relatable characters, diverse families, and timeless Scripture, A. S. Gates creates stories that connect biblical lessons to the everyday challenges children face: bullying, fear, loneliness, and change.

David and the Really Big Problem is the first book in the Stories by A. S. Gates collection. More stories are coming soon.

A. S. Gates believes every child deserves to hear: You are seen. You are loved. And you are never alone.
